About This Game 

        

          
          
Grandpa left you his backyard. The county left you a tax bill. You have 30 days to dig up enough gold to keep the house, and you're doing it with your friends, a rusty shovel and zero safety training.

          
Backyard Gold Rush is a 1–4 player co-op digging game. Tear up the yard one block at a time, haul buckets of dirt to the creek, pan for gold, then haggle it away at the pawn shop for better tools. Every run is a new yard. Every day the taxes go up. Every tunnel might fall on your head.

          
Dig. Pan. Get rich. (Maybe.)

          
    
            
  • A fully diggable yard: topsoil, clay, gravel, grandpa's secret mine, then the deep mine full of quartz, jadarite and diamonds.
    • 
            
  • Panning minigame: carry your bucket to the creek and wash it for nuggets, junk and weird artifacts.
    • 
            
  • The pawn shop: sell your finds, catch flash sales and upgrade shovels, buckets and pans.
    • 
            
  • 30-day runs: pay the tax every deadline or the county repossesses. Random yard, shareable seeds and a daily yard with a leaderboard.
    • 
          

          
Failing together is half the fun

          
    
            
  • Cave-ins: dig a room too big and the ceiling comes down. Prop it with timber or dig your friend out before the air runs out.
    • 
            
  • Proximity voice chat: hear your friends scream as the tunnel collapses.
    • 
            
  • Ghosts and revives: die and haunt your team until someone brings you back. Life insurance is available. Probably a good idea.
    • 
            
  • Funny end-of-run awards: obituaries, a police report and awards for your worst decisions.
    • 
          

          
Grandpa's town has… options

          
    
            
  • The Arsenal: guns, ammo and explosives. No questions asked.
    • 
            
  • Skip the taxes and the police raid your yard, with SWAT, K-9s and flashbangs.
    • 
            
  • The casino: a wheel of fortune, roulette and slots. Bet the tax money. What could go wrong?
    • 
            
  • Plus: rails and an ore cart, flooding and water pumps, thunderstorms, Old Hollis the nosy neighbor, a cursed idol, a gym, chess by the campfire and cosmetics you earn from awards.
    • 
          

          
Built for Friday nights: short sessions, cheap price, maximum chaos. Host a public lobby, or invite friends privately with a 5-digit code.

        

      


      

        
Mature Content Description 

        
The developers describe the content like this:
This game contains cartoon violence against people, including firearms, explosives and police raids, with blood and bodies shown in a low-poly art style. It includes simulated gambling (a wheel of fortune, roulette and slot machines) that uses only in-game money: no real money can be spent or won.
